## Products

- Avatars: Turn selfies into photorealistic single and multiplayer avatars, without
  any AI training. On-demand image creation with text prompts, for messaging apps,
  next-gen emojis, and talent agencies monetizing digital IP.
- Fashion Photoshoot: Fashion brands create photorealistic AI models of any race or
  body type wearing any outfit in any scenario, saving photoshoot cost and time.
  AI training is optional.
- Fashion Try-On: Brands integrate the Try-On API on their website or styling app.
  Shoppers of any body type upload a picture and try on any outfit instantly, to
  increase conversion and reduce returns. No AI training required.
- Ad Creator: With one selfie and a context image, Easel generates personalized
  ads. Ad platforms integrate Easel's API to create client ad content faster.
- Product Photoshoot: With one image, brands place products in any scenario while
  preserving granular detail. Add a selfie to generate people-product visuals.
